Install through Azkar’s signed repository to receive updates from your package manager.

Debian and Ubuntu · APT

These commands add the signed stable repository. For Dev, change stable to dev in the repository line. 

curl -fsSL https://jamhour1g.github.io/Azkar-App/keys/azkar-repo.asc | gpg --dearmor | sudo tee /usr/share/keyrings/azkar-repo.gpg >/dev/null
echo 'deb [signed-by=/usr/share/keyrings/azkar-repo.gpg] https://jamhour1g.github.io/Azkar-App/apt stable main' | sudo tee /etc/apt/sources.list.d/azkar.list
sudo apt update
sudo apt install azkar
Fedora · DNF

These commands add the signed stable repository. For Dev, change stable to dev in the repository name and URL. 

sudo rpm --import https://jamhour1g.github.io/Azkar-App/keys/azkar-repo.asc
sudo tee /etc/yum.repos.d/azkar.repo >/dev/null <<'EOF'
[azkar-stable]
name=Azkar Stable
baseurl=https://jamhour1g.github.io/Azkar-App/rpm/stable/
enabled=1
gpgcheck=0
repo_gpgcheck=1
gpgkey=https://jamhour1g.github.io/Azkar-App/keys/azkar-repo.asc
EOF
sudo dnf makecache
sudo dnf install azkar

Arch and other distributions: Use the AppImage above. If it does not launch, mark it executable in Properties or run chmod +x azkar-*.AppImage and ./azkar-*.AppImage. Arch may also need fuse2: sudo pacman -S fuse2.

Explore Azkar features

Read, build a daily routine, and keep the passages that matter to you within reach.

Keep your place in your daily reading.

Find a passage when you need it

  • A library ready to explore Browse the bundled adhkar by collection, topic, or search.
  • Understand what you read Keep Arabic, translations, explanations, and source references together where available.
  • Keep useful passages handy Save favorites and copy text to return to or share later.

Build a recitation routine

  • Read one passage at a time Move through a collection in a focused recitation session.
  • Track your repetitions See your progress through each collection as you recite.
  • Return to your place Resume a session, revisit a passage, skip ahead, or start the collection again.

Bring your own adhkar into your daily routine.

Make room for what you read

  • Create your own collections Bring existing passages together or add your own remembrances.
  • Keep the meaning and source Add and edit text, translations, explanations, and references for your own passages.
  • Make a collection recognizable Choose a cover image and arrange its passages in the order you prefer.

Find your way back quickly

  • Put regular collections first Pin them, change their order, or show them on Home.
  • Keep related passages together Use tags to find topics across your library.
  • Keep your library tidy Rename tags, check where they are used, and restore deleted tags when needed.

Keep remembrance close while you get on with your day.

Reminders from your own library

  • Choose what you want to remember Use collections or individual passages as reminder sources.
  • Fit reminders around your day Choose an interval, weekdays, and a daily time window, or keep them running continuously.
  • Keep the selection fresh Follow library order, randomize your queue, or give some passages more weight.
  • Stay in control Favorite a passage from its reminder card, pause reminders from the tray, and choose a comfortable card size and reading duration.

Know when your next prayer is due

  • See today’s prayer times Home shows your schedule and a countdown to the next prayer.
  • Choose the alerts you need Turn individual prayer alerts on or off directly from Home.
  • Use your local schedule Find your city or enter a location, with calculation settings available when you need them.

Read comfortably. Keep your desktop clear.

A reading experience that fits you

  • English and Arabic Switch the interface language, with right-to-left layout for Arabic.
  • Light, dark, or system theme Choose the appearance that feels comfortable on your screen.
  • Text you can read comfortably Adjust text size and typeface, and see the result in a live preview.
  • Your preferred reminder language Choose reminder text independently of the interface language.

Ready when you need it

  • Keep reminders running in the background Close the main window and reach Azkar through the system tray.
  • Start with your desktop Enable start at sign-in on supported installed desktop environments.
  • Free and open source Use Azkar on Windows, macOS, and Linux, with preferences stored on your device.
